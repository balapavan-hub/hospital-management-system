Deployment Pipeline

Onboarding & Operational Workflow

How organizations integrate and collaborate on MediConnect India.

Step 1: Hospital Registration

Organizations register online, providing credentials and licensing credentials to verify clinical authenticity.

Step 2: Platform Verification

Platform Super Administrators audit the credentials to approve registration and secure tenant authenticity.

Step 3: Tenant Provisioning

Approved hospitals receive a secure, dedicated tenant space and provision the initial Hospital Admin credentials.

Step 4: Workforce Setup

Hospital Administrators register clinicians, nurses, pharmacists, and laboratory staff under dedicated roles.

Step 5: Patient Engagement

Patients search partner networks, schedule appointments, and access digital prescriptions securely.

Step 6: Integrated Healthcare Delivery

Prescriptions, billings, and laboratory reports move instantaneously inside the secure central pipeline.
